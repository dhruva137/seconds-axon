# Sources

Numbers match the superscripts in the concept brief ([docs/Seconds_Concept_Brief_axon.pdf](docs/Seconds_Concept_Brief_axon.pdf)). Every link was checked on 17 Nov 2026.

1. **Deccan Herald**, "Excess food? Here's how to make sure it is not wasted". Bengaluru Solid Waste Management Ltd figures (about 296 tonnes of food wasted a day), Robin Hood Army, piggeries, the smell test.
   https://www.deccanherald.com/india/karnataka/bengaluru/excess-food-here-s-how-to-make-sure-it-is-not-wasted-3302413
2. **Bengaluru Homeless Survey Report**, via WebJosh. 14,189 homeless people counted; 1,869 shelter places.
   https://webjosh.com/article/bbmp-west-zone-has-highest-concentration-of-homeless-people-says-report%C2%A0-1719069801-19451
3. **Citizen Matters**, shelters after DAY-NULM funding lapsed.
   https://citizenmatters.in/?p=86452
4. **Duke University study, via The Quint**: about 2,000 slums in Bengaluru, while government records list 597.
   https://www.thequint.com/videos/watch-omidyar-study-reveals-2000-slums-in-bengaluru-govt-says-only-597
5. **University of Agricultural Sciences Bengaluru** wedding-waste study (2012), via Covai Post. 943 tonnes a year across 531 halls.
   https://www.covaipost.com/?p=88649
6. **FSSAI, Save Food Share Food**, food shelf-life guidance: cooked food 4–6 hours at room temperature, 12–24 hours refrigerated. The live site was unreachable on 17 Nov 2026, so this is the Internet Archive copy.
   https://web.archive.org/web/20260720035947/https://sharefood.eatrightindia.gov.in/guidance-for-row-food.html
7. **Harvard Food Law and Policy Clinic and Global FoodBanking Network**, India legal guide executive summary (no liability protection for food donors).
   https://www.foodbanking.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/India-Executive-Summary-05.29.20.pdf
8. **Solid Waste Management Rules 2026**, summary via Mondaq (in force 1 April 2026; bulk generators over 100 kg a day).
   https://www.mondaq.com/india/waste-management/1762618/whats-new-under-the-solid-waste-management-rules-2026
9. **Business Standard**, Bengaluru's waste charge for bulk generators (up to ₹12 a kg).
   https://www.business-standard.com/india-news/bengaluru-solid-waste-management-fee-bbmp-125040100420_1.html
10. **FSSAI, Recovery and Distribution of Surplus Food Regulations 2019** (donor records; registration of distribution organisations).
    https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Press_Release_Gazette_Notification_Surplus_Food_06_08_2019.pdf
